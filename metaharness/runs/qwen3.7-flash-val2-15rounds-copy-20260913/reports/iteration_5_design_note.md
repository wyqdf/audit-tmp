# Iteration 5 — act_ledger_audit (design note, pre-eval)

**Where iteration 4 left the loss.** On the multi-unit task: 55 correct / 45 wrong, 16
strict subsets and 0 supersets, 58 samples with multi-unit gold of which only 23 are
correct. Two off-line measurements over iteration 4's own wrong samples say what is left
to win:

- Of 45 wrong samples, 22 have a gold unit missing from the draft. 15 of the 45 named
  that unit in `considered` and dropped it; the other 30 never named it. Re-opening what
  was named is worth at most 15 samples and iterations 3 and 4 both measured ~0 repaired.
- Of those 22, the missing unit is in the training vocabulary at all for 11, and appears
  in the top-3 units ranked by *this question's* reference support for 1. The closest
  reference case that answers with more parts than the draft contains the missing unit
  for 1. No retrieval- or evidence-ranking change can name these units.

So the failure is upstream of everything the check could say: the candidate set itself is
generated by picking among vocabulary units, and the traces show the model comparing.
`ex0009` treats `赌博` and `开设赌场` as two names for the same conduct and keeps the
more specific; `ex0014` cites the profile's shape line ("usually 1 part") to answer with
one element of `窝藏、包庇`. Meanwhile the check *oscillated* on `ex43 s1`: the symmetric
specificity note reads as a reason to switch in either direction, the model switched 16
times and lost the sample to the 32-call budget.

**Change.**

1. **Ledger before units.** The solver first writes a ledger of the question: one entry
   per separate act or component its facts establish, each with the establishing fact and
   the unit it calls for, plus the entries it rules out (marked `-`, so being considered
   is visible). Two units naming the *same* act at different generality are one entry;
   two units naming *different* acts are two entries and both belong in the answer. The
   system prompt, the skill and the forced-check middleware all carry this.
2. **The check audits coverage, not spelling.** `check_answer(draft, ledger, considered)`
   reports every entry whose unit is not in the draft, with the reference case that
   answers it (or the rule that an unlisted unit is not a reason to drop it), and quotes
   the closest reference case that answers with more parts than the draft when an entry is
   open. Naming what the model never named is the point; the iteration-3/4 `considered`
   audit stays, now secondary.
3. **The corpus shape stops licensing a short answer.** Both the profile and the check
   report the part distribution as a rendering fact and say the count follows from the
   ledger, not from the usual count. The question-type width push is not used when a
   ledger is given (iteration 4 added it and the free-form task lost 4 samples / won 2).
4. **The check cannot oscillate.** A unit relation is reported once per question; if the
   mirror relation comes back, the tool says it reads the same either way and cannot
   settle it, and from the third call it says so explicitly. Single-part answers (every
   reference answer one unit) invert the coverage rule: entries are alternatives for that
   one slot, never parts to add.

**Hypothesis.** Generating the candidate set from the question's own acts, and auditing
the draft against that ledger instead of against the reference unit list, raises the mean
validation score above the 0.584 of iteration 3, mainly by converting strict-subset
answers into complete ones on the multi-unit task, without regressing the single-part and
free-form tasks.

**Falsifiers to look for in the results.**
- Supersets appear where there were none: the ledger over-generates and the specificity
  wording is admitting units no act establishes.
- The multi-unit task is unchanged although entries were reported as open: the model is
  building the ledger after deciding, and the ledger has to move *before* the choice.
- The single-part task regresses: the single-slot branch is not suppressing additions.
- `call_budget` errors persist or rise: the check is still inviting repeated calls.
